01

Brand foundation

Built for the people
behind the performance.

The platform exists to reduce the distance between ambitious professionals and the teams that need their expertise. Every decision should make that connection clearer, safer and more human.

A

Brand purpose

To connect people with the right opportunity, while giving teams a clear view of the expertise required to move a programme forward.

“A career should be easier to discover than the programme that needs it.”
B

Brand promise

  • Clear opportunity
  • Verified expertise
  • Human relationships
  • Global access
C

Positioning

Motorsport career infrastructure for talent, teams and the people who build the sport.

Talent↔Teams↔Opportunity
D

Brand attributes

PrecisionAmbitionPerformanceAuthenticityCommunityProgress
Brand vision

Make the paddock more reachable.

Every specialist should be able to find a meaningful role. Every team should be able to find the right person without relying on fragmented networks. The platform provides the structure that makes those connections possible.

Brand mission

Connect capability with consequence.

We create a professional, trusted environment where drivers, engineers, mechanics, strategists, analysts, media professionals and team operators can discover, evaluate and pursue meaningful work.

Current inconsistency

The original platform used multiple visual conventions.

Early versions mixed high-impact product cards with smaller dashboard components, inconsistent button treatments and several unrelated accent uses. The system now standardises these elements around one career-first hierarchy.

Recommended standard

One clear system, not many visual styles.

Use the established red, bronze and dark surface palette. Avoid unrelated gradients, neon accents and decorative colour usage. Every visual element should support a job, team, profile or career decision.

UX principles

Design decisions
with clear intent.

These principles guide every new screen and component. They protect clarity while preserving the energy of motorsport.

01

Clarity over decoration

Every visual treatment should make a career decision easier to understand.

02

Motorsport without noise

Use speed, movement and technical language without gimmicks or excessive effects.

03

Data should feel human

Numbers are contextualised with job, team and career meaning.

04

Precision in every detail

Keep status, salary, location, deadlines and requirements consistent.

05

Career first

Navigation and content should support the next meaningful professional action.

06

Real people, real opportunities

Use authentic team and profile language. Avoid artificial startup language.

07

Speed without chaos

Use concise layouts and progressive disclosure for complex information.

08

Trust by design

Privacy, verification and clear data context are visible product features.

04

Visual identity

Professional by design.
Dynamic by nature.

The visual language combines technical precision with the energy of a race environment. It is deliberate, restrained and built for long reading sessions.

Brand voice

Professional.
Human. Precise.

Brand voice is a decision system, not a tone filter. It protects the product from generic startup language.

Professional ↔ Casual

Prefer professional clarity. Use conversational language only where it supports the user.

Technical ↔ Simple

Explain technical terms, but never remove meaningful context.

Confident ↔ Humble

State what the product does without overstating what it guarantees.

Energetic ↔ Calm

Use movement and energy in the interface, not in the language.

Corporate ↔ Human

Speak to people, not to an internal management structure.

Developer guidelines

Build from the system.
Do not recreate it.

New components should introduce a reusable token, state and responsive rule. One-off styles require a documented reason.

Structure

Keep layout, component and page-specific styles in separate files. Reuse tokens where possible.

Tokens

Use semantic names. Never hard-code repeated colours, spacing or radii.

Components

Implement visual and state variants through the same component interface.

Responsive

Define breakpoints before content. Do not add fixed widths for mobile layouts.

Accessibility

Use semantic HTML, labels, focus styling and accessible status messages.

Data

Keep job and profile fields consistent across all views and components.

Motion

Use reduced-motion checks and avoid animation that blocks interaction.

Testing

Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard and screen-reader behavior.
